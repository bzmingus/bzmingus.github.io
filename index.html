Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information our apps collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

TL;DR: Brayne Games does not collect, store, or share any names, email addresses, passwords, or any personally identifying details from users.

1.Advertising (AdMob)

Our apps use Google AdMob to display both personalized and non-personalized ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language, country)
  • Advertising ID
  • Approximate location (country or region)
  • App usage data related to ad performance (impressions, clicks, etc.)

If you allow personalized ads, Google may use your device’s advertising ID and app activity to show more relevant ads. If you choose non-personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to you.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2.EU/EEA Consent (GDPR)

Users in the European Union, EEA, and UK are shown a consent form provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Before personalized ads or analytics are used, EU users can choose to:

  • Allow personalized ads
  • Allow non-personalized ads
  • Allow or reject analytics tracking
  • Reject all non-essential tracking

Your choices are stored on your device and can be changed at any time through your system settings (resetting the advertising ID resets consent). If consent is not given, the app will only serve non-personalized ads and essential services.

3.Children’s Privacy

Our apps are intended for general audiences but are not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4.Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties outside of Google AdMob’s / Firebase services. Data collected by Google services (AdMob, Firebase) is governed by Google’s own privacy policies.

5.Data Security

We do not store any personal information on our servers. All analytics and advertising data is processed by Google’s secure systems.

6.Your Choices

You may:

  • Opt out of personalized ads using your device settings
  • Reset your Android Advertising ID at any time
  • Decline analytics and personalized ads if you are in the EU/EEA
  • Uninstall the app at any time to stop all data collection

7.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The updated version will always be posted within the app or on the app store listing.

8.Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at admin@BrayneGames.com.
