Bryan's Mahjong Party

Available on the App Store

Four mahjong games on one table, hosted by Bryan, who took the job for the money and would like you to know it.

Download on the App Store
Mahjong Solitaire

The classic tile-matching game with a tray. Tap a free tile and it hops up to the tray; set its match beside it and the pair slams together and shatters. The tray holds five, so you can line up your next moves instead of hunting for them.

Mahjong Slide

Every tile is face up. Tap two matching tiles that can see each other along a row or column and they clear. When no pair is in sight, slide a tile to push the others into line. Five board sizes and a daily board for each size with a Game Center leaderboard.

Mahjong Nines

A two-player duel against Bryan. Nine columns for you, nine for him. Push a tile into the top of one of your columns and whatever pops out the bottom is the next play. Fill every column with four of a kind and the table is yours.

Tile-A-Whirl

Nines with a pool of tiles spinning in the middle and a dozen aliens mixed in to gum up the works. Draw from the pool, feed your columns, and first to fill every column wins.

Tile sets

Play with real mahjong tiles or swap in animals, birds, sea life, dinosaurs, food, fruit, garden, toys, transport, famous paintings and sculptures, a gallery of characters, and the aliens.
