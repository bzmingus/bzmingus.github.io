Bryan's Mahjong Party

Available on the App Store

Four mahjong games on one table, hosted by Bryan, who took the job for the money and would like you to know it.

Download on the App Store
Mahjong Solitaire

The classic tile-matching game with a tray. Tap a free tile and it hops up to the tray; set its match beside it and the pair slams together and shatters. The tray holds five, so you can line up your next moves instead of hunting for them.

Mahjong Slide

Slide, match, and clear the board! Drag tiles along rows or columns, pushing other tiles into place to line up matching pairs with only empty space between them. Each slide must create a match, so plan your moves carefully. Choose from five board sizes, or take on a fresh daily board for each size and compete on the Game Center leaderboard.

Mahjong Nines

Think you can beat Bryan? Race to fill your columns with four of a kind in this head-to-head tile game. Push a tile into the top of a column, then play whatever pops out the bottom. Complete your table before Bryan does and victory is yours!

Tile-A-Whirl

Take Nines for a spin! Draw from a whirling pool of tiles and race Bryan to fill your columns with four of a kind. A dozen aliens are mixed in to gum up the works, so completing your table won’t be quite so simple. Beat Bryan to the last match and the win is yours!

Tile sets

Play with real mahjong tiles or swap in animals, birds, sea life, dinosaurs, food, fruit, garden, toys, transport, famous paintings and sculptures, a gallery of characters, and the aliens.
