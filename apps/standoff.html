Standoff

Place every piece on the board so that nobody can capture anybody. A perfect standoff — or no deal.

Queens

One queen in every color region — but no two queens may share a row, a column, or touch diagonally. Simple rules, devious boards.

Rooks & Bishops

Same idea, new arsenal: fill the regions with rooks and bishops without leaving a single piece in another's line of attack.

Features

  • Multiple board sizes and difficulty levels
  • Assists for learners: see exactly which squares a piece threatens
