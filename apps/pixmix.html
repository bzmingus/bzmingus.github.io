Three picture games, one app. Hundreds of original images to unscramble, uncover, and explore.

Art Remix

The picture has been chopped up and shuffled. Slide and swap the tiles to put the artwork back together.

Pixplosion

The picture explodes into a 3-D ball of shards. Spin the wreckage until you catch it from exactly the right angle — and suddenly it's a picture again. Every other angle? Just shards.

Big Picture

One enormous scene, chopped into a grid of tiles and scrambled. Swap the tiles to rebuild it — pieces lock in as you place them right, until the whole panorama comes together.

A real Big Picture panorama with a few tiles out of place. Drag a tile onto another to swap them — right placements lock in. Scroll to find them all.
