Bryan's Hangman

Available on the App Store

Bryan's Hangman is the word game you played on paper, with Bryan standing in for the stick figure. Pick letters from the keyboard. Each right letter fills in the word. Each wrong letter draws another piece of him onto the gallows. Seven misses and the round is over. Finish the word first and he drops down and runs off, which he treats as a personal triumph.

Download on the App Store

The words are everyday ones by default, the kind everybody knows. Turn on Word Nerd Mode in Settings and the whole dictionary opens up, with words from six to thirteen letters. Over eleven thousand words in all, so the game stays fresh.

The letters are real wooden pieces on a felt table, or felt letters on a wooden one. Three tables to choose from. Sounds can be turned off in Settings.

Bryan is the brain in a tie who hosts the game. He is not thrilled about this one. He will stand there anyway.

Free. Made for iPhone and iPad.
