Bryan's Pin Spin

Available on the App Store

Pin Spin is a one-thumb timing game. A wheel spins, pins ride on it, and you fire the next pin up from the bottom. Land it in a gap and it joins the wheel. Touch a pin that's already there and the level restarts.

Download on the App Store

The first wheels are slow and open. Then they fill up, speed up, change speed without warning, and reverse mid-round. Every few levels the wheel changes the rules: thread one ball through a single gap, land only inside the moving green light, hit a pin on its tip, or stack pins on top of each other.

Levels are unlimited. Bryan is the brain in a tie who hosts the game. He won't be impressed by your score, but he'll keep count anyway.

Made for iPhone and iPad.
